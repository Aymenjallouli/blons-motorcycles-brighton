The workshop

From L-plates to flame-painted customs.

Blons Motorcycles is a repair and servicing workshop on Little Western Street in Brighton. Commuter scooters, learner bikes, adventure bikes, superbikes and old customs all end up on the same red lift, and riders keep mentioning the same thing: the work is explained clearly and priced fairly.

  1. 01

    Servicing & oil changes

    Routine services and oil changes for scooters and motorcycles, done in the workshop.

  2. 02

    Repairs

    Mechanical repairs, with the problem and the fix explained in plain English.

  3. 03

    Tyres

    Tyre changes, awkward ones included. One rider’s niche bike, with discontinued tyres, was sorted within three hours.

  4. 04

    Brakes

    Brake work right down to freeing a stuck caliper piston, a job one Honda rider called “fast service and well priced”.
